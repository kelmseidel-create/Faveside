Privacy Policy

Faveside is a creator-discovery and organization service. This policy explains the information we handle and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide, such as name and email address.
  • Your saved creators, preferences, child-profile names, and creator approvals.
  • Launch-list email addresses and limited technical information used to prevent abuse.
  • Basic server records needed for security and reliability.

How we use information

We use information to operate Faveside, synchronize your choices, provide parent-managed experiences, communicate requested updates, prevent abuse, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information.

Children and parent controls

Faveside Parent Controls are intended to be configured and managed by an adult account holder. Children should not create their own accounts or submit personal information. Parents should use only a nickname or first name for a child profile and should not enter sensitive information.

Service providers

We may rely on hosting, email, payments, analytics, and creator-platform providers to operate the service. They receive only the information needed to perform their services and are governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the service, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and security. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from launch updates through a message you receive. You may request access, correction, or deletion of account information by contacting Faveside. Some records may be retained when required for security or legal reasons.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Faveside develops. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions may be sent to hello@faveside.com.
